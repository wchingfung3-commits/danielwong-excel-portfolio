Project 04 · Business presentation

Executive KPI Presentation & Report Redesign

A six-slide management presentation that turns synthetic operating data into a concise performance story with editable charts and clear actions.

Deck length6 slides
Native charts3
Format16:9
Editing100%

Objective

The project demonstrates how scattered management figures can become a short, decision-focused presentation for an executive audience.

Approach

  • Organized the storyline around performance, regional growth and customer retention.
  • Converted synthetic source figures into editable native PowerPoint charts.
  • Used a consistent hierarchy so each slide communicates one main point.
  • Finished with specific Q4 priorities linked to the analysis.

Outcome

The final deck provides a clear six-slide reporting structure that a business can update each quarter. Text, figures and charts remain editable for practical handover.
